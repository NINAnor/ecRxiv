## How to contact us

**Bug reports**

Please submit Bug reports as [issues](https://github.com/NINAnor/ecRxiv/issues/new)

**Indicator review or feedback**

To provide simple feedback or comprehensive reviews on any of the indicators on ecRxiv, 
create a new issue with [this form](https://github.com/NINAnor/ecRxiv/issues/new?template=feedback-or-comments.yml).

**General queries**

Please direct emails to post@ecRxiv.com
